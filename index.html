Start narrow. Prove it works. Then expand.

We begin with one repetitive ticket category, so your team can judge quality, speed and effort saved without changing your support operation overnight.

  1. Pick one repetitive category

    Onboarding questions, integration setup, configuration issues or recurring how-tos. We look at last month's tickets together and choose the one that repeats most.

  2. We draft the replies

    Using your help centre, internal docs and the way your team has answered before, we prepare a draft for each new ticket in that category.

  3. Your team reviews and sends

    Agents approve, edit or discard each draft. We track what they changed and report weekly on what is genuinely worth automating.

Common questions

Do you replace our support team?

No. We prepare drafts for repetitive tickets and your agents decide what gets sent. The aim is to give them time back for complex customer issues.

Do we need to change our help desk?

No. The pilot runs alongside the help desk and process you use today. We agree the exact setup together on the intro call.

Do you automate every ticket type?

No. We start with one repetitive category and only suggest expanding where the weekly reports show quality is consistently strong.

What happens after the pilot?

We review the thirty days together and you decide whether to continue, expand to another category or stop. There's no obligation to carry on.
